# B04 — Discover all supported screens and rows

**Status:** [x] Complete
**Domain:** `foundation`
**Dependencies:** [B02](./B02.md), [B03](./B03.md)
**Dependency gate now:** [x] Satisfied

## Outcome

Generalize OCR navigation to menus, nested tabs, scroll lists, popups and account readers; use available in-game Battle History/stat exports; validate screen geometry and locale; distinguish locked, unseen, unavailable, maxed and unreadable.

## Acceptance gate

Recorded screens across supported unlock stages resolve stable IDs without fixed row ordering; new tabs do not shift a purchase to a different target.

**Proven by**, in `tests/test_screen_discovery.py`:

- `test_a_second_unlock_stage_resolves_the_same_screen_ids` — seven further captures resolve the expected screen IDs, including the first recorded evidence for `battle.defense` and `battle.utility`.
- `test_every_claimed_unlock_stage_pair_really_shows_different_rows` — reads both captures of every pair the matrix claims and requires the row sets to differ, so the table cannot be padded with re-captures.
- `test_a_capture_that_is_not_stage_evidence_is_not_claimed_as_stage_evidence` — `in_run_early` parses to exactly `in_run_lit`'s rows and is deliberately excluded from the stage table.
- `test_a_tap_belongs_to_exactly_the_row_that_produced_it` — each row's tap is inside that row and inside no other, on all ten recorded upgrade screens.
- `test_an_upgrade_keeps_its_identity_and_its_own_target_across_stages` — a shared upgrade keeps its identity while its price is read per stage.
- `test_an_uncatalogued_row_on_a_younger_account_is_named_but_never_tappable` — an unresolved label is reported, never targeted.

and in `tests/test_account_screens.py`, for the nested menu:

- `test_the_stats_control_exists_only_on_the_settings_panel` — the Settings → Stats control is `located` on Settings and `absent`, not ambiguous, on the panels it leads to.
- `test_a_separate_capture_of_one_panel_reads_the_same_values` — pins the account re-captures as re-captures, so they cannot later be mistaken for a second stage.

and in `tests/test_autopilot.py`, for the tab clause:

- `test_a_fourth_tab_fails_closed_instead_of_tapping_the_wrong_one` — the in-run tab bar is the one index-derived coordinate in the bot; it refuses a layout that is not three cells.

`capabilities()['uncatalogued_labels']` records three rows the game shows and
the catalog cannot name: Attack Range and Unlock Multishot Upgrades on the
second-stage Attack page, Unlock Thorn Upgrades on Defense. The reader already
refuses them a tap. Naming them is a catalog change, which alters what the bot
may buy, so it belongs to the catalog task rather than to screen discovery.

deliberately refused rather than parsed; reading it belongs to U01.
