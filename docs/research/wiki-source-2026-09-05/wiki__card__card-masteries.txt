Card Masteries are a late-game upgrade layer for your cards in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense. Once unlocked, each mastery adds an extra effect to a specific card, but only while that card is equipped in your loadout.

Card Mastery Overview
Card Mastery
Card Masteries become available once you have maxed 30 cards and reached the Card Mastery Milestone at Tier 16, Wave 100. From that point you can unlock masteries individually on the cards page.
Each mastery costs a varying amount of stones to unlock. Unlocking it also opens that card's Mastery Labs, where the effect can be levelled further. Keep in mind that a mastery only applies while the card it belongs to is in your active loadout — unequip the card and you lose the effect.
