Thorn Damage is a workshop upgrade that punishes anything that hits your tower. When an enemy makes contact, it takes damage equal to a percentage of its own maximum health, which makes thorns especially effective against high-health targets.

Description
Thorn Damage deals a percentage of the attacking enemy's maximum health back to it whenever it hits your tower. This applies to ranged attackers as well as melee enemies, not just things touching the wall. Bosses take half the listed percentage, so a high thorns value still matters against them but at reduced effect.
Workshop
You unlock Thorn Damage in the workshop for 500 coins once you have both Defense % and Defense Absolute. From there you can buy 99 upgrades, each adding 1% thorns, up to a maximum of 99% (which works out to 49.5% against bosses).
A few mechanical points worth knowing: thorn damage does not trigger lifesteal, and your tower does not need to actually take damage for thorns to fire. Protectors are a counter to watch for, since they cut thorn damage by 60% for all enemies within their range.

Garlic Thorns
Garlic Thorns is a lab upgrade unlocked at tier 2, wave 1000, with 10 upgrade levels. Once researched, Vampire elite enemies take a percentage of your thorns damage per second while they are using their ranged attack, giving you a way to pressure enemies that would otherwise sit safely at range.
