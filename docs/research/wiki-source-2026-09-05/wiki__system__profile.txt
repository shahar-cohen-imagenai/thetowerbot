Description
The player profile is a menu built for showing off. Its purpose is to let you display your achievements to other players while you compete in tournaments, rather than to change anything about how your tower performs.
Accessing
To open your profile, tap the tower shown on the home menu. That takes you straight into the profile screen where you can review and edit what others see.
Display
The profile lets you pick one tower skin and five relics to show off. These choices are purely cosmetic: displaying a relic or skin here does not activate it or grant its effects, so you can show anything you own without affecting your build.
The menu also lists your competitive milestones, including your highest tournament ranking and your highest tier and wave reached. If you want a different identity on the leaderboards, you can change your tournament name from this screen as well.
Since v26.0, your profile also shows the guild you are currently active in, so other players can see your affiliation at a glance.
