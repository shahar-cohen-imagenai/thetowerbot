Version 0.28 introduced Dissonance, a per-tier difficulty modifier that disables a chosen workshop tab in exchange for a stat boost, alongside Bot Bot, five unlockable Bot+ abilities, and the Synchronicity pathing upgrade. It also added Overheat conditions to shorten high-wave runs and tournaments, and a rotating relic section in the Event Store. The release was followed by hotfixes that corrected Dissonant Boost values after the coin bonus shipped higher than intended, settling at 5x for Attack, Defense, and Ultimate Weapons and 3x for Utility.

Reproduced from the developer's release notes.
Hey Tower Defenders! We are excited to share our v28 update with all of you. We are rolling out the build on Android now, and iOS will be rolling out within the next two day. This update should be fully available for all players by Thursday. Additionally, we will be enforcing a version lock for the tournament at the end of the week. Here is a look at the changes:
Introducing: Dissonance! This is a new layer to Tier gameplay that presents a fun challenge, and a bonus to help push for further milestones. Dissonant Runs represent a new difficulty setting that can be applied to a Tier. Doing so disables a selected workshop tab (Attack, Defense, Utility, or Ultimate Weapons). Based on how far you progress, the related stat is boosted for that specific tier.
The stats are:
Attack Workshop → Attack
Defense Workshop → Health
Utility Workshop → Coins
Ultimate Weapon Workshop → UW Damage
Dissonance is unlocked at Tier 4 Wave 90. Once unlocked, you will have access to select this modifier for all unlocked Tiers. At Tier 17, Wave 60, you'll unlock four Dissonant Echo labs for each workshop type (Attack, Defense, Utility, Ultimate Weapon). These labs apply a percent of each tier's Dissonant Boost to your Tower. Unlocking Dissonance rewards you with a starting 0.5% Echo boost. Click on the Dissonant Boosts section on the Battle menu for an expanded panel.
Bots are getting a lot of attention this update! To start, we have moved the Bots menu to its own tab in the Events page. This allows us more space to expand the feature, like so:
A new Bot has entered the arena: Bot Bot! This new bot increases the effects of other bot effects within the radius, and has four stats: Range, Cooldown, % Increase, and Duration.
Bots are getting an upgrade! The Bot+ feature gives each bot a new specialized ability. Bot+ is available when all Bots are unlocked. Each bot has its own Bot+ ability, which requires a one-time purchase using stones to unlock. Once the ability is unlocked, it can be upgraded further using medals. If you opt to respec your bots, all medals will be returned as usual, even if invested in the Bot+ stats. Unlocking the bot again will give you access to your Bot+ ability (without needing the stone purchase again).
The five new Bot+ abilities are Wildfire, Titan Shock, Bonus Cells, Echoing Shot, and Maximum Power. Descriptions can be found in the Changelog below.
Unlocking all five Bot+ abilities grants you access to Synchronicity , a stone upgrade that allows you to assign bots to have synchronized pathing. Unlocking Synchronicity grants you two slots, with each additional slot costing stones. Bots can be freely assigned into these slots so long as you have them unlocked.
We have added in a new mechanic that amps up the difficulty of your run at higher waves. When you have successfully cleared Wave 4500 on any Tier, you will see an Overheat button on your Battle screen. This panel will define when the Overheat conditions will occur, and what the Overheat conditions are. This feature is designed to reduce the overall duration of a run in tiers and tournaments. This isn't a "game end" state when reaching this wave, but be prepared to face the heat!

For normal tiers, the only change is that the Enemy Skip Decay Overheat condition kicks in at a defined wave. The panel will appear once you've cleared Wave 4500, but won't go into effect until much later in your run. Check the Heat button on each Tier more information.
Overheat will appear in tournament runs as well, and can be viewed in the existing Heat button. We have added 4 new Overheat Conditions that will only appear in tournaments:
Damage Decay: Tower Damage is reduced every 10 waves
Health Decay: Tower Health is reduced every 10 waves
More Fleets: An additional Fleet spawns every 100 waves
More Elites: An additional Elite spawns every 5 waves
We will be introducing a new Relic section in the Event Store starting on April 14th, 2026. This section will feature older relics from previous runs. Each week will feature four relics: two Rare and two Epic. These relics cost gems: 600 gems for Rare relics, and 1200 gems for Epic relics. This section of the Event Store will feature free and premium event relics earned from completing event missions, excluding most relics earned via medals. After a period of time, event relics will be added into the pool of options.
Note : This section rotates every week, so make sure to check it at the beginning and middle of an active event!

We've identified an issue with our Dissonant Bonus values upon release that is granting a larger coin bonus than expected. While we recognize that this is a difficult change and we never intend to take away power from the players, these adjustments are necessary to maintain the competitive integrity we had imagined. We will be adjusting this value live; however, this unfortunately affects our other workshop bonuses. Here is a look at what will immediately change, and what the intended end goal is: Dissonant Run's maximum bonuses will be decreased from 5x to 2x. This effect will be visible for all players on the current build. In our upcoming v28.0.3 update, we will fix the maximum bonuses for Attack, Defense, and Ultimate Weapon to return to its original 5x bonus. While we do not want to make the change to all areas, we want to address the coin earnings issue and not create a larger earnings gap. To summarize:
Temporary adjustments to Dissonant Run maximum bonus: Attack, Defense, Utility, and Ultimate Weapons are set to 2x
v28.0.3 adjustments: Utility at 2x, Attack, Defense, and Ultimate Weapon at 5xWe are finalizing content for our v28.0.3 update now, and hope to deliver additional improvements next week. Additional content that will be covered in the upcoming hotfix includes adjustments to stunning effects overtime, improvements on our Bot+ abilities, a fix for Flame Bot's Damage Reduction, and UI improvements for Dissonant Runs. We appreciate your patience and understanding on this one, and hope everyone is having fun exploring the new features.
Hey pals. I wanted to come back and give you a little more context on what happened, because you deserve it. When we were in late beta, we made a decision to increase the Attack, Defense, and UW bonuses. The coin bonus was always meant to stay at 2x - it just got caught up in that change when it shouldn't have. We missed that, and it shouldn't have gone live, which is why we had to do the quick correction. We hear you, and we are bringing the Utility bonus up to 3x, and will be giving a gift of gems to all players as well. This means all four bonuses will be at 3x in the current live version. Here's where things are headed:
Attack, Defense, and UW bonuses are coming back to 5x in v28.0.3, which we're aiming to get out next week.
That same update includes Bot+ improvements, the Flame Bot DR fix, stun adjustments, and Dissonant Run UI improvements.I've read every comment. I see what you're saying, and I'm bringing it all back to the team. We will have more info in the upcoming Tower Tea. Thank you for being here. Sam.
