Ranged Enemy labs are a set of research options that weaken the ranged enemy type during your rounds. Each lab targets one stat — health, attack or range — and applies its reduction at all waves once researched.

Description — Ranged Enemy Attack
This lab is also unlocked through Milestones at tier 9, wave 20. It reduces how much damage ranged enemies deal at all waves, which helps your tower survive their hits. It has 30 levels.
Description — Ranged Enemy Range
This lab unlocks at tier 13, wave 80. It shrinks the attack range of ranged enemies, so they have to get closer before they can fire on your tower. It has 30 levels, with the per-level values listed in the table below.

Description
All three Ranged Enemy labs modify the stats of ranged enemies while a round is running. One side effect worth knowing: researching these labs breaks the default stat ratios between enemy types. For example, boss health is normally a fixed multiple of basic enemy health, but once you have researched basic enemy health reductions, that multiple no longer holds true. Keep this in mind when comparing enemy stats or planning around specific breakpoints.
