Keep in mind that some abbreviations have more than one meaning. Always check the context to make sure the definition matches how the term is being used.

Some acronyms in this game carry multiple meanings depending on the situation. When you come across an abbreviation, look at the surrounding context to confirm which definition applies before assuming.
Ultimate Weapons (UW)
When you see a "+" attached to an Ultimate Weapon abbreviation, it refers to the Ultimate Weapon Plus (UW+) ability. UW+ abilities are unlocked with stones after you have unlocked all 9 Ultimate Weapons. The number that follows the "+" indicates the level of that UW+ ability, starting at 0 when first unlocked.
You may also see extra letters appended to a UW abbreviation. These suffixes point to a specific stat belonging to that Ultimate Weapon. The table below lists the known suffixes and what they stand for.

Cards
A "#" or "m" after a card abbreviation refers to that card's Mastery. Card Mastery becomes available for purchase with stones once you reach Tier 16 Wave 100 and have maxed all cards. The number after the symbol indicates the lab level of that Mastery, starting at 0 at base unlock.

Workshop (WS)
A "+" after a Workshop stat abbreviation points to the Workshop Enhancements (WS+) version of that stat. Not every Workshop stat has a WS+ counterpart, and some WS+ upgrades do not correspond to a base Workshop stat.
