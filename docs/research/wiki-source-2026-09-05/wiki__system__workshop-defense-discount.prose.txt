Workshop Defense Discount is a game system that reduces how many coins you spend on defense upgrades in the workshop. If you regularly invest coins into defensive stats, this discount stretches those coins further over time.
Description
Workshop Defense Discount lowers the coin price you pay when upgrading the defense category in your workshop. Every level of the discount makes each defensive upgrade cheaper than it would otherwise be, so the same pile of coins buys more upgrades.
This matters most once defense upgrades become a meaningful part of your coin spending. The discount applies to the cost itself rather than refunding you after the fact, so you simply see lower prices in the workshop.
