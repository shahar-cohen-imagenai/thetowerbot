Version 0.22 added the module system, unlocked through Milestones at Tier 2, Wave 90. Players could equip modules in four categories—Cannon, Armor, Generator, and Core—each with a main stat and random sub-stats, plus unique effects on Epic modules. The update also raised the Def% limit from 90% to 98% and fixed bounce shot performance problems and Ranged attacks not charging Berserk.
