Workshop Enhancements are a late-game upgrade layer that sits on top of your normal Workshop stats. Each one applies a growing multiplier to a specific stat, letting you push damage, survivability, and economy far beyond what the base Workshop offers.
Enhancements are split into the same three categories as the Workshop itself: Attack, Defense, and Utility. You unlock new enhancements within a category by spending coins on the ones you already own in that same category, so progress in each track is self-funded.

Workshop Enhancement
Workshop Enhancements mirror the structure of the standard Workshop, with separate Attack, Defense, and Utility tracks. Each track contains its own set of enhancements, and every enhancement is a multiplier you level up with coins.
Unlocking new enhancements is category-specific: the coins you spend on Attack enhancements count toward unlocking the next Attack enhancement, and the same applies to Defense and Utility. Spending in one track does nothing for the others, so you need to invest in all three if you want every enhancement available.
Unlock
Workshop Enhancements become available once you reach tier 12 wave 60 and have completed the Workshop Enhancement lab. Until both conditions are met, the enhancement menu stays locked.
Once unlocked, the first enhancement in each category is free, and further ones open up as you sink coins into that category. The cost table below shows what each level costs.
Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Attack Enhancement — Damage
Damage is the free starting Attack enhancement, available as soon as Workshop Enhancements are researched. It begins at a x1 multiplier and gains 0.01x per level, capping at 400 levels for a total of x5 damage.

Rend Max unlocks after you have spent 50 billion coins on Damage enhancements. It multiplies Rend Max, its chance, and its multiplier, starting at x1 and gaining 0.01x per level. It caps at 400 levels for a x5 bonus.

Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Orb Size — Cash Bonus
Cash Bonus is the free starting Utility enhancement, unlocked once the Workshop Enhancement labs are done. It multiplies your Cash Bonus, starting at x1 and gaining 0.01x per level, with a 400-level cap for a x5 bonus.
On top of the multiplier, each level of this enhancement also raises cash per wave, interest percentage, and the max interest per wave amount by 1% each, making it a broad economy boost rather than a single-stat one.
