Version 0.12 added the Recovery Package utility upgrades and the Intro Sprint card, along with a new Tier 4 lab research for respeccing the workshop. The end-of-round stats panel was expanded to track things like death wave bonuses, golden tower gains, waves skipped, and real round time. Smaller fixes included the demon mode duration display, workshop buy multiplier support, and wave-skip coins no longer inflating the round-end coin stat.
