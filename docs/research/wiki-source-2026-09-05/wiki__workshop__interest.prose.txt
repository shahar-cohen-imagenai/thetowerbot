Interest rewards you for holding onto cash. At the end of each wave, you earn a percentage of the cash you still have on hand, so money you don't spend keeps working for you. It sits in the Workshop Upgrades category and is supported by two related lab researches.

Overview
Interest pays out a percentage of your unspent cash when a wave ends. The larger the balance you carry into the end of a wave, the more interest you collect, which encourages saving rather than spending everything immediately.
Workshop
Interest/Wave is bought in the workshop for 5000 coins once Free Upgrades is unlocked. It has 99 levels starting from a 0% base, and each level adds 0.06% to that base, reaching a maximum of 5.94%. The payout is worked out after Cash Per Wave is applied, using the formula: (Current Cash + Cash/Wave × Cash Bonus) × Interest/Wave %.
