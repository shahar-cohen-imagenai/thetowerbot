Second Wind is a defensive mechanic that gives you another chance when your tower would otherwise fall. Two labs in the Labs tree build on it: Second Wind Blast adds a clearing effect when it triggers, and Recharge Second Wind lets the effect come back after a set number of waves instead of being spent for the rest of the run.
Labs — Second Wind Blast
Second Wind Blast is unlocked through Milestones at tier 3 wave 150. Once researched, triggering Second Wind also wipes out a percentage of the enemies that have spawned, thinning the wave that overwhelmed you and giving your tower room to recover.
The lab has 4 levels, each raising the share of spawned enemies the blast clears. The exact percentages per level are listed in the data table below.
Labs — Recharge Second Wind
Recharge Second Wind is unlocked through Milestones at tier 14 wave 60. Normally Second Wind is a one-time save, but this lab allows it to recharge and become available again a set number of waves after it activates.
The lab has 7 levels, improving how quickly Second Wind comes back online. Check the data table below for the recharge values at each level.
