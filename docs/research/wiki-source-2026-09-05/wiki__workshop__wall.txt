The Wall is a defensive layer that stops enemies from reaching your tower until its health is depleted. Once destroyed, it rebuilds automatically over a set period, but enemies can pass through freely during that rebuild window. It is one of the later defensive unlocks in the workshop and becomes a major part of your defense setup once you invest in its upgrades.

Overview
The Wall acts as a physical barrier between enemies and your tower. Enemies cannot reach the tower while the Wall has health remaining. Once its health drops to zero, the Wall is destroyed and begins rebuilding over time. During the rebuild, enemies are not blocked and can reach the tower as normal.
When the Wall finishes rebuilding, it pushes all enemies away from the tower, ensuring nothing is left standing inside the Wall's perimeter when it comes back up.
Workshop
The Wall is unlocked in the workshop for 500 million coins, available after Death Defy. Unlocking it gives you access to two workshop upgrade tracks: Wall Health and Wall Rebuild.
Workshop — Wall Health
Wall Health sets the Wall's maximum HP as a percentage of your tower's health. It starts at 20% and gains 0.1% per upgrade level, with 1800 total upgrades bringing it to a maximum of 200% of tower health.
Wall Rebuild
Wall Rebuild controls how long the Wall takes to come back after being destroyed. It has 300 upgrades, each shaving 2 seconds off the rebuild timer, down to a minimum of 600 seconds from upgrades alone. The hard cap on rebuild time is 150 seconds, which requires additional reductions from labs to reach.

Lab
All Wall labs are unlocked through Milestones. Wall Health and Wall Rebuild become available at tier 8 wave 10. Wall Regen and Wall Thorns unlock at tier 10 wave 70. Wall Invincibility unlocks at tier 12 wave 10. Wall Fortification unlocks at tier 14 wave 60.
Wall Health adds to the workshop value, while Wall Rebuild reduces the workshop rebuild value. Wall Regen applies a percentage of tower regen to the Wall, Wall Thorns lets the Wall deal a percentage of tower thorns when attacked, Wall Invincibility grants a brief invulnerable window after each rebuild, and Wall Fortification lets the Wall hold health beyond its maximum as long as Wall Regen is active.
