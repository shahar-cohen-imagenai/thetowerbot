Health is the maximum hit points of your tower — the raw pool of damage you can absorb before a run ends. It is one of the core defensive stats you will invest in from the very start of the game, and it underpins the broader concept of Effective HP (eHP) used to measure real survivability.

Description
Health sets the maximum HP of your tower. Every point of damage you take is subtracted from this pool, and the run is over when it reaches zero.
Workshop
Health is available in the Workshop for free from the start, unlocked alongside Health Regen. There are 6000 workshop upgrade levels for Health, making it one of the deepest coin sinks in the game. Beyond the workshop itself, you can push Health further through the health lab and the Health card.

Cards
The Health card multiplies your workshop Health by its card multiplier. It stacks multiplicatively with your other multipliers, so its value grows as the rest of your Health scaling improves.
Effective HP (eHP)
Health is the foundation of Effective HP, the measure of how much total damage enemies must deal to bring you to 0 HP. The basic formula is:
eHP = (Health + Defense Absolute) / (1 - Defense %)
Other modifiers feed into this, including the Chrono Field Damage Reduction lab, Chrono Field Reduction % and Packages. With Chrono Field Reduction accounted for, the formula becomes:
eHP = (Health / (1 - Chrono Field Reduction %) + Defense Absolute) / (1 - Defense %)
The result tells you the damage an enemy must deal to kill you in a single hit. When Packages are involved, use your highest achievable health value rather than your base maximum. For example, if your fully modified max HP is 1,000 and max Packages give 16.5x (max workshop, no modifiers), you can reach 16,500 HP — so use 16,500 in the formula, unless you take so many hits that you cannot reliably hold the full package amount.
