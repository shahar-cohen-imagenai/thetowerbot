Tiers are the difficulty levels of The Tower: Idle Tower Defense. The game has 24 tiers in total, and climbing to a higher tier means facing tougher waves for greater rewards.

Description
Tiers represent the difficulty of the game, ranging from tier 1 up to tier 24.
You unlock the next tier through Milestones by reaching 100 waves on your current tier. The requirement increases at the top end: tiers 16 through 21 each ask for 300 waves on the previous tier before they open up.
List of Tiers
