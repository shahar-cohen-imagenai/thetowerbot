Thunder Bot is one of the bots you can buy in The Tower. When it activates, it stuns enemies in an area around it, then applies a slowing linger effect once the stun ends. Both the stun and the linger are only 50% effective against Bosses and Elites, so keep that in mind when planning around tough waves.

Description
Thunder Bot stuns enemies in an area around the bot when it activates. Once the stun ends, affected enemies are slowed by a linger effect. Both the stun and the linger are 50% effective against Bosses and Elites.
Unlocking/Upgrading
Like all bots, Thunder Bot is unlocked and upgraded through the Event Shop menu. The medal cost to unlock it depends on how many other bots you have already bought. Each of its three upgrades starts at a base cost of 100 medals and rises by 40 medals per level.
Duration
The Duration upgrade increases how long enemies are stunned. It has 20 levels.
Cooldown
Cooldown is the time between the bot's activations. It starts at 120 seconds and the upgrade has 15 levels.
Linger
The Linger upgrade increases the slow percentage of the linger effect. It starts at 20% and has 20 levels.
Range
The Range upgrade increases the area the bot covers while active. Range is amplified by your tower range, so it scales with your tower's range stat. It has 15 levels.
