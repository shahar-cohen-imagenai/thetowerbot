Workshop Utility Discount is a workshop upgrade that cuts the coin cost of your utility upgrades. If you find yourself saving up for expensive utility purchases, this discount helps those coins stretch further.
Description
Workshop Utility Discount reduces how many coins you spend on utility upgrades in the workshop. Each level of the discount shaves more off the price, so the savings grow as you invest in it.
This only applies to the utility section of the workshop — it does not affect attack, defense, or other upgrade categories. If utility upgrades are a regular part of your build, the discount pays for itself over time by letting you afford higher levels sooner.
Lab
The Workshop Utility Discount lab becomes available once you reach wave 60 on tier 2 in Milestones. It has 99 levels, giving you plenty of room to keep pushing the discount higher as you progress.
