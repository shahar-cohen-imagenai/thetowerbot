Currency
Overview of The Tower's currencies: Cash, Coins, Gems, Power Stones, Medals and Elite Cells — how you earn and spend each one.
Elite Cells
Elite Cells are a currency dropped by Elite enemies in The Tower, used to buy lab boosts. Learn how to earn and increase them.
Gems
Gems are a premium currency in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense. Learn every way to earn them and what to spend them on.
Keys
Keys are a currency earned from Legend Tournament placements and spent on unique Tower upgrades in the Vault's Tech Trees.
Medals
Medals are a currency earned from events and missions in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense, spent in the Event Shop on items like Gems, Bots and Relics.
Power Stones
Power Stones are a premium currency in The Tower used to buy and upgrade Ultimate Weapons. Learn how to earn or purchase them.
Shards
Module Shards are a currency in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense, used for the game's module system.
Tokens
Tokens are a currency in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense earned from Weekly Challenges and Guild Chests, spent in the Guild shop.
