Scatter Labs are a set of lab researches that target Scatter enemies, one of the enemy types you face during rounds. By investing in these labs, you can directly reduce how hard Scatters hit, lower how much health they have, and make your Chain Lightning progressively stronger against them.

Description — Scatter Enemy Health
Unlocked at Tier 19 Wave 60, this lab cuts the health of Scatter enemies by a percentage per level, letting you kill them faster. It has 30 levels to research.
Description — Lightning Amplifier - Scatter
You unlock this lab through Milestones at Tier 16 Wave 60. It increases the damage Chain Lightning deals to Scatter enemies with each consecutive hit on them. Description
Scatter labs change how Scatter enemies behave and interact with your defences during rounds. One side effect to be aware of: once you start researching enemy stat labs, the usual base relationships between enemy types stop holding true. For example, a Boss will no longer always have exactly 20 times the health of a Basic if you have researched Basic health reduction.
