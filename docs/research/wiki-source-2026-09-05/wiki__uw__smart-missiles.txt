Smart Missiles are an Ultimate Weapon that periodically fires a volley of homing missiles at enemies. Each missile tracks its target and explodes on impact, dealing damage based on a multiplier of your tower's damage — and because that multiplier accounts for your crit and super crit factor and chance, the weapon scales with the rest of your build.
Beyond the basic workshop upgrades, Smart Missiles have one of the largest lab trees of any Ultimate Weapon, letting you extend missile lifetime, stack damage on repeat hits, add area-of-effect explosions, and unlock a manually triggered Missile Barrage.

Description
Smart Missiles are an Ultimate Weapon. Once activated, they launch a set number of missiles on a repeating timer. The missiles home in on enemies and detonate when they connect.
The damage each missile deals is a multiplier of your tower damage. That calculation includes your crit and super crit factor and chance, so anything that raises those stats also raises your missile output.
Workshop — Basic Upgrades
Like every Ultimate Weapon, Smart Missiles' basic upgrades sit in the Ultimate Weapon menu once you've unlocked the weapon itself. From there you can spend stones on three tracks: Damage, Quantity, and Cooldown.
Damage has 30 levels, Quantity has 15 levels, and Cooldown has 16 levels. Maxing all three tracks costs 24,407 stones in total; the per-level breakdown is in the cost table below.

Lab — Missile Amplifier
Missile Amplifier makes repeated hits on the same enemy progressively stronger: each missile that lands boosts the damage of the next missile striking that target, and the bonus stacks additively. It's especially valuable against tough, long-lived targets that soak multiple volleys. This research has 25 levels.

Lab — Missiles Explosion
Missiles Explosion is a single-level research that turns every missile impact into an area-of-effect blast, damaging enemies around the target rather than just the target itself. The starting blast radius is 0.30. Completing it unlocks the Missiles Radius research.

Lab — Missile Barrage
Missile Barrage is a single-level research that adds a manually activated ability: once per run, you can fire a barrage of missiles all at the same time, with a base amount of 20 missiles per activation. You trigger it yourself during a round, so you can save it for a dangerous wave. Completing it unlocks the Missile Barrage Quantity research.

Lab
Smart Missile lab researches are gated behind Milestones. Missile Despawn Time, Missile Amplifier, Missiles Explosion, and Missiles Radius all unlock at tier 7 wave 30. Missile Barrage and Missile Barrage Quantity unlock later, at tier 8 wave 50. Recharge Missile Barrage is separate, unlocking at tier 14 wave 60.
