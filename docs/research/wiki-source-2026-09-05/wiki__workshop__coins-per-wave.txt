Coins Per Wave is a workshop upgrade that pays you a flat amount of coins every time you complete a wave. Because it triggers on every wave regardless of kills, it provides a steady baseline of income that scales with how far you push in a run.

Description
Coins Per Wave awards coins each time you finish a wave. Unlike kill-based income, it pays out simply for surviving a wave, so it keeps earning even against low-density waves or when your damage comes from sources that do not generate many kills.
Workshop
You unlock Coins Per Wave in the workshop for 100 coins, and it requires Coins/Kill Bonus, which itself sits behind Cash Bonus and Cash/Wave in the upgrade chain. The upgrade has 149 levels starting from a base value of 1, and each level adds 1 more coin per wave, topping out at 150 coins per wave. The payout is further boosted by the All Coins perk and by any multipliers shown in the tier coins bonus screen, so its real value in a run is higher than the workshop number suggests.

Coins/Wave
The Coins/Wave lab is unlocked through Milestones by reaching tier 1 wave 30. It has 99 levels, and each level raises the increment that workshop levels provide, which also raises the maximum workshop value. The per-level costs are shown in the table below. This lab is what turns Coins Per Wave from a minor trickle into a meaningful income source over long runs.
