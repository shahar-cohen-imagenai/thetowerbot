Ultimate Crit is an epic card in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense. It gives your ultimate weapons a chance to critically hit, using your tower's existing crit factor for the damage calculation.
Ultimate Crit
When equipped, Ultimate Crit adds a percentage chance for your ultimate weapons to deal critical damage. The size of that critical hit is not set by the card itself — it uses your Tower Crit Factor, so anything that raises your crit factor also makes these ultimate crits hit harder.
The card matters most once you rely on ultimate weapons for a large share of your damage. Before that point, the crit chance it provides has little to multiply, and other cards will usually contribute more to your run.

Card Mastery
Unlocking the Ultimate Crit mastery costs 750 stones. Once unlocked, it increases the crit chance your ultimate weapons gain from the card.
The mastery is not a permanent account-wide bonus. It only takes effect while Ultimate Crit is in your active loadout, so you need to keep the card equipped to benefit from the extra crit chance.
