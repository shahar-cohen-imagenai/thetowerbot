Rend Armor is a late-game Workshop upgrade that adds a chance-based damage amplification mechanic to your tower. When it triggers on an enemy, that enemy takes increased damage from your projectiles for the rest of the run. It only affects tower projectile damage, so it does nothing for damage coming from other sources.

Description
Rend Armor gives your tower a chance to apply a damage multiplier to enemies it hits. The multiplier applies to individual enemies rather than globally, and it only increases damage from tower projectiles. Damage from any other source is unaffected.
Workshop
Rend Armor is unlocked in the Workshop for 500,000,000,000 (500 billion) coins, and requires Super Critical to be completed first. Unlocking it opens up two new upgrade tracks: Rend Armor Chance and Rend Armor Mult. When fully stacked on an enemy, Rend Armor can increase the damage that enemy takes by up to 800% of normal.
Rend Armor Chance
Rend Armor Chance determines how often a projectile hit applies the Rend effect to an enemy. Once applied, the damage increase on that enemy is permanent for the rest of the run. There are 299 levels available. Each level adds 0.10% to the trigger chance, starting from 0.10% and reaching a maximum of 30.00%.
Workshop — Rend Armor Mult
Rend Armor Mult controls how much extra damage an affected enemy takes per stack. There are 299 levels, each adding 0.10% (0.0010X) to the multiplier. It starts at 0.0010X and caps at 0.3000X per level investment. The total Rend effect on a single enemy can stack up to a limit of 800.00%. Only tower projectile damage is modified by this multiplier.
