Version 0.7 expanded the upgrade pool with new stats for missiles, Chrono Field, Swamp, Golden Tower, land mines, and orb speed, giving players more ways to shape their builds. It also introduced target priorities and additional round stats for deeper run information. The daily gem gift moved to an 8 AM availability, late-game performance was significantly improved, and bugs like the death ray rotation stall and Second Wind animation failures were fixed.
