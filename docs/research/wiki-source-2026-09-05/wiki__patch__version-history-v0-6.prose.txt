Version 0.6 focused heavily on rebalancing defensive strategies, buffing health scaling, regen, and absolute defense while nerfing lifesteal, which had become overpowered. Waves past roughly 60 now scale harder to keep rounds from dragging past an hour, and enemies deal ramping damage on consecutive hits. Android players got cloud saves and back button support, alongside iOS and tablet UI improvements. Smart missiles were improved with better range adaptation and higher missile counts, plus various bug fixes and tournament tweaks.
Change list
Reproduced from the developer's release notes.

Waves starting at roughly wave 60 and above will have higher scaling for difficulty to end rounds a bit earlier (more health, slightly faster spawn rate, slightly higher chance of tanks and fast enemies). The general reason for this is to shorten rounds across the board as it was never designed to run for an hour plus. It will also help balance defensive builds as below, expect more changes though
