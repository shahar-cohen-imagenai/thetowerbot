Spotlight is an Ultimate Weapon that projects rotating beams around your tower. Any enemy caught inside a beam takes increased damage from your other sources, making it a force multiplier rather than a standalone weapon.

Description
Spotlight creates one or more beams that sweep in a circle around your tower, each covering a set number of degrees. Enemies standing in the light take multiplied damage.
The multiplier applies to all damage sources except those based on a percentage of enemy health or those that scale off wave stats.
Workshop — Basic Upgrades
Spotlight is bought and upgraded from the Ultimate Weapon menu using stones. Once unlocked, you can level three stats: Bonus (the damage multiplier), Angle (how wide each beam is) and Quantity (how many beams rotate around the tower).
Bonus has 25 levels, Angle has 60 levels and Quantity has 3 levels. Fully upgrading all three costs 42,236 stones in total; the per-level breakdown is in the cost table below.

Workshop — Ultimate Weapon Plus
Spotlight's Ultimate Weapon Plus ability adds a further boost to its damage bonus, scaling as a percentage of damage per meter. Your Tower Range affects this scaling, so range investments feed directly into the bonus.
Like all UW+ abilities, it only becomes available after you have purchased all nine Ultimate Weapons.
Labs — Spotlight Missiles
This lab lets the tower fire missiles from the Spotlight on a timed cycle. Missile damage is taken from your Smart Missiles damage; if you have not unlocked Smart Missiles, the missiles use a base damage of 14x instead.
The lab has 18 levels. When it first appears in milestones, the display shows a 20-second firing interval, but no missiles actually fire until you buy the first level, which sets the frequency to 19 seconds.

Labs — Spotlight Coin Bonus
This lab adds a coin multiplier to enemies that die while inside the Spotlight beam. The kill itself does not have to come from Spotlight damage — the enemy only needs to be in the light when it dies.
