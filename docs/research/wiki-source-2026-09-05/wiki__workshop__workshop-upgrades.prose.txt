The Workshop is where you spend coins on permanent upgrades for your tower, separate from the cash upgrades you buy mid-run. Once you progress far enough, it also houses Workshop Enhancements, a second layer of multipliers that sit on top of your existing stats.
This page covers how the Workshop and its Enhancements are structured, when they unlock, and what each individual enhancement does.

Workshop
The Workshop offers three categories of upgrade: Attack, Defense and Utility. You buy them with coins between rounds, and anything purchased this way is permanent, unlike the cash upgrades bought during a round, which reset when the battle ends. If you want to redo your coin spending, a Workshop Respec will refund it.
The Workshop menu is also where you unlock and upgrade Ultimate Weapons.
Workshop Enhancement
Workshop Enhancements mirror the same Attack, Defense and Utility split as the base Workshop. Each enhancement is a multiplier you level up with coins, and new ones within a category become available by sinking coins into the other enhancements of that same category.
Unlock
Workshop Enhancements become available once you reach tier 12 wave 60, and you also need to have finished the Workshop Enhancement lab research. The cost table below lists what each level costs once the feature is open.

The Damage enhancement comes free as soon as Workshop Enhancements are researched. It begins at a 1x multiplier and gains 0.01x per level, capping at 400 levels for a total of 5x. The level-by-level costs are shown in the table below.

Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Attack Enhancement — Rend Max
Rend Max opens up after you have spent 50 billion coins on damage enhancements. Unlocking it applies a 1x multiplier to Rend Max, its chance and its mult, and each level adds 0.01x. It runs to 400 levels, topping out at a 5x bonus.
Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Attack Enhancement — Critical Factor
Critical Factor requires 500 billion coins spent on enhancements before it unlocks. It starts by adding 1.0x to your Crit Factor and grows by 0.1 per level, with 400 levels bringing it to a maximum of 5x.
Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Attack Enhancement — Damage/Meter
Damage/Meter unlocks once 5 trillion coins have gone into attack enhancements. The multiplier starts at 1x and rises by 0.01x per level, reaching a 5x bonus at its cap of 400 levels.
Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Attack Enhancement — Super Crit Multi
Super Crit Multi is gated behind 50 trillion coins spent on enhancements. It adds 1.0x to your Super Crit Multi when unlocked and increases by 0.1 per level, maxing out at 400 levels for a 5x multiplier.

Workshop Enhancement Unlocks — Orb Size — Cash Bonus
Cash Bonus is free once the Workshop Enhancement lab is complete. Beyond raising the Cash Bonus multiplier from 1x by 0.01x per level, each upgrade also adds 1% to cash per wave, interest percentage and the max interest per wave. It caps at 400 levels for a 5x bonus.

Coin Bonus unlocks after 50 billion coins spent on Utility enhancements. It starts at 1x and gains 0.01x per level, capping at 200 levels for a 3x bonus.
