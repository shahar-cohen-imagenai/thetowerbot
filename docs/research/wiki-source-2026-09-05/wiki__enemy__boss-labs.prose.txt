Boss labs are a pair of lab researches that weaken boss enemies during your rounds. Both are unlocked through Milestones at tier 9 wave 20, and each one targets a single boss stat across every wave you face.

Description — Boss Attack
Boss Attack is a lab you unlock in Milestones at tier 9 wave 20. Researching it reduces the attack of boss enemies at all waves, so bosses deal less damage to your tower throughout the round. Like Boss Health, it has 30 levels.
Description
Boss labs directly modify the stats of boss enemies while a round is running. One thing to keep in mind: once you start researching labs that change enemy stats, the usual base relationships between enemy types stop holding true. For example, boss health will no longer reliably sit at twenty times basic enemy health if you have also been researching basic health reductions, so treat those baseline multipliers as a starting point rather than a fixed rule.
