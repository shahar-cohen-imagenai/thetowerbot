Module labs are a set of lab researches focused on your modules. They cover how often bosses drop common and rare modules, how many shards you earn from various sources, and how much it costs to level modules up. Later labs also let you unmerge modules, gain more from shattering, and ban unwanted effects when rerolling.
Each lab is unlocked through Milestones at a specific tier and wave, and most have multiple levels that stack their effect as you research them.

— Module Coin Cost
This lab is unlocked in Milestones at Tier 10 Wave 40 and lowers the coin cost of levelling up modules.
It has 30 levels and provides a 30% reduction to coin costs when fully researched, pairing naturally with the Module Shard Cost lab to make upgrades cheaper overall.

— Unmerge Module
This lab is unlocked in Milestones at Tier 8 Wave 20 and has a single level. Once researched, it lets you reverse module merges.
How an unmerge resolves depends on how the module was made. If the merge required a duplicate epic+ module, unmerging returns the lower-level module along with that epic+. If the merge accepted any module of the right rarity, unmerging instead converts the module into shards of the unmerged type. The table below covers the specifics.
