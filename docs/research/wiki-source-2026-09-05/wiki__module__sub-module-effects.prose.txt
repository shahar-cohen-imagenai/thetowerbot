Every module in The Tower can carry Sub-Module Effects, which are additional stat rolls attached to the module. The rarity of each sub-module is rolled separately from the module's own rarity, so an Epic module can hold a Rare or even a Common sub-module.
Sub-modules can be rerolled with reroll shards if you want different effects. You can lock a sub-module you want to keep so that slot is excluded from the reroll, but locking raises the shard cost. One important limit: bonus stats from Assist Module sub-module effects do not break through hard caps. Caps such as 98% defense, 90% Chrono Field speed reduction, 150s Wall rebuild, 7s Shockwave Frequency and 37s Inner Land Mine cooldown still apply no matter how many sub-module bonuses you stack.

Sub-Module Effects are available on all modules, and each sub-module's rarity is independent of the module it sits on. An Epic module can roll a Rare or Common sub-module, and vice versa.
You reroll sub-modules with reroll shards. Locking a sub-module protects that slot from being rerolled, but each lock increases the cost of the roll. See the Modules page for the broader submodule mechanics.
Keep in mind that extra stats granted by Assist Module sub-module effects cannot push a stat past its hard cap. The caps that matter here include 98% defense, 90% slow on Chrono Field speed reduction, 150s Wall rebuild, 7s Shockwave Frequency and 37s Inner Land Mine cooldown.
