Coins Per Kill (CPK) is a Workshop upgrade that raises the coins you receive every time you destroy an enemy. Because it multiplies with several other coin bonuses, it is one of the core ways to grow your coin income as you progress.

Description
Coins Per Kill increases the coins awarded each time you destroy an enemy. Every kill in a run pays out based on this value, so even small increases add up quickly over thousands of kills per round.
Workshop
You unlock Coins Per Kill in the Workshop for 100 coins, buying it with Coins/Wave once you have purchased Cash Bonus and Cash Per Wave. It starts at a base value of x1.00 and has 149 levels, each adding 0.01 to the multiplier, up to a maximum of x2.49.
The multiplier stacks multiplicatively with every other bonus that boosts coins from kills, such as the Tier Bonus, Skin Bonus, Golden Bot and Golden Tower. None of these change the coin/kill figure shown in game. The All Coin perk is the exception — it does raise the displayed Coin/Kill value.
Formula
The full calculation is: Coins/Kill = (Workshop × CPK lab + CPK mod effect) × (1 + coin bonus enhancement) × (1 + coin bonus perk base of 0.15 × perk quantity) × (trade-off perk of 1.8 × (1 + trade-off lab)).
Work through the formula without rounding at any step, since rounding will make your result differ from the in-game display. Note that the enhancement is counted twice overall — once in the tier bonus and once in CPK itself. If you have not unlocked the trade-off perk, use 1 in place of that term.
