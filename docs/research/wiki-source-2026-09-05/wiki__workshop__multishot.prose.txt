Multishot is a workshop upgrade that gives your tower a chance to fire at more than one enemy each time it shoots. It is split into two separate upgrades: Multishot Chance, which controls how often the effect triggers, and Multishot Targets, which controls how many enemies are hit when it does.

Description
Multishot gives your tower a chance to fire at multiple targets with a single shot. When the effect activates, your tower fires additional projectiles at enemies in range, letting you clear groups of enemies faster than single-target fire alone.
Workshop
Multishot is unlocked in the workshop for 400 coins once you have purchased Damage/Meter. Unlocking it gives you access to two separate upgrades: Multishot Chance and Multishot Targets, each of which can be levelled independently.
Multishot Chance
Multishot Chance determines how often the multishot effect triggers when your tower fires. The workshop offers 99 levels for this upgrade, with each level adding 0.50% to the activation chance. Fully maxed, this gives you a 49.50% chance for multishot to activate on any given shot.
Multishot Targets
Multishot Targets sets how many projectiles your tower fires when multishot activates, provided enough enemies are in range. It starts at 2 targets and has 7 levels, with each level adding 1 additional target, up to a maximum of 9 targets.
