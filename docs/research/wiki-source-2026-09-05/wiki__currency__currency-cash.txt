Cash
Cash is the in-run currency used to buy temporary upgrades while a run is active. It doesn't carry over between runs — you earn it, spend it, and start fresh next time.
Earning Cash
You earn Cash during a run from several sources: defeating enemies, completing waves, the Cash / Wave upgrade, and Interest as a form of passive income.
Each enemy has a base value of $1. That value rises by $1 every 10 waves up to wave 200, then the growth slows to $1 every 20 waves. The table below shows how enemy value scales.

Cash Flow + How to Improve it
At the end of every wave, two systems pay out Cash automatically: Cash per Wave and Interest. Understanding how they interact is key to keeping your upgrades funded in longer runs.
Cash per Wave
Cash per Wave is a flat payout you receive automatically when a wave ends. It scales with your upgrades and how far you've progressed, and it's always applied first at the end of a wave — before Interest is calculated.
Interest
Interest pays you a percentage of the Cash you're currently holding, rewarding you for keeping unspent Cash on hand. There's a cap on how much Interest you can gain per wave, so hoarding past that point earns you nothing extra.
Because Cash per Wave is added before Interest is worked out, the flat payout can push your balance up into a higher Interest payout if you were sitting below the cap.
Improving Cash Flow
How much Cash you have depends heavily on how deep into a run you get, so investing in Cash Flow early pays off later. You can improve it through:
- Cash Bonus upgrades (Workshop and Lab) - Cash / Wave upgrades (Workshop and Lab) - Interest / Wave upgrades (Workshop and Lab) - Max Interest upgrades (Lab) - The Cash card (Cards) - The Enemy Balance card (Cards) - Golden Tower (Ultimate Weapon) - Cash Bonus or Cash/Wave sub-module effects (modules)
Cash Bonus
The Cash Bonus enhancement unlocks for free once you complete the Workshop Enhancement Labs. Unlocking it raises your Cash Bonus multiplier by x1, and each level after that adds 0.01x. The enhancement also boosts Cash per Wave, Interest percentage, and Max Interest per wave by 1% per upgrade. It caps at level 400, giving a 5x bonus — see the level table below for the full progression.
