Amplify Bot
Amplify Bot boosts damage against enemies in its range. Learn its upgrades, duration, cooldown, bonus, range, and lab research.
Bot Bot
Bot Bot is a support bot that amplifies the effects of other bots within its range. Learn its upgrades, labs and costs.
Flame Bot
Flame Bot is an unlockable bot in The Tower that burns enemies around it, dealing damage over time and reducing the damage burned enemies deal.

Thunder Bot
Thunder Bot stuns enemies in an area, then slows them with a linger effect. Learn its upgrades, labs, and how it works.
