Milestones are one-time rewards tied to your progression. As you push to higher waves on each tier, you unlock currencies, labs, cards, themes, and relics automatically.
You do not need to do anything special to earn them beyond reaching the required tier and wave. Once a milestone is available, the Milestones button shows how many rewards are waiting to be claimed.

Description
Milestones are progress-based rewards that unlock as you reach specific tiers and waves. They include currencies, labs, cards, themes, and relics.
Rewards
When you hit the required tier and wave, the Milestones button displays the number of unclaimed rewards. Tap it to collect everything you have earned.
Tier 23 milestones include lab unlocks for each Fleet and its respective Ultimate Battle Condition. The exact order these labs unlock in is not confirmed.
