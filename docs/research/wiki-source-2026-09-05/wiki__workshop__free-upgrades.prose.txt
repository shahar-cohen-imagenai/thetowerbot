Free Upgrades give you a chance each wave to level up a workshop stat without paying for it. There are three separate chances — one each for Attack, Defense and Utility — and each one only applies to upgrades in its own workshop.

Free Upgrades
Overview
After each wave in a round, each of your three Free Upgrade chances rolls independently. On a success, a stat in the matching workshop gains a level for free. Free Upgrades never pick a stat that is already maxed, so a successful roll is never wasted as long as something in that workshop can still be upgraded.
If the Wave Skip card triggers, each workshop's Free Upgrade can roll one extra time for every wave skipped. Chances above 100% also roll over: at 110% Free Utility Upgrade, for example, you are guaranteed one free level and have a 10% chance of a second that wave.
Workshop
The Free Upgrades upgrade is bought in the workshop for 800 coins, and sits behind Coins/Kill Bonus and Coins/Wave in the unlock order. Buying it unlocks three separate tracks: Free Attack Upgrade, Free Defense Upgrade and Free Utility Upgrade.
Each track has 99 levels, and every level adds 0.5% to that workshop's free upgrade chance, for a maximum of 49.5% per workshop from the workshop alone.

Card and Perk
The Free Upgrades card and the free upgrade perk each raise all three workshop chances by the value shown on them, on top of your workshop levels. With everything combined, the highest free upgrade chance you can reach is 90.75%.
Free Upgrades
