Protector labs are a set of three lab researches that weaken protector enemies during your rounds. Each one targets a different protector stat, and all three apply at every wave once researched.

Description — Protector Health

Description — Protector Radius
Protector Radius is unlocked through Milestones at tier 9 wave 20, the same requirement as the other protector labs. Researching it shrinks the radius of protector enemies at all waves, which reduces the area their protection covers. It has 30 levels.
Description — Protector Damage Reduction
Protector Damage Reduction is also unlocked at tier 9 wave 20 in Milestones. Its levels cut the damage reduction that protector enemies have on all waves, so your damage dealers lose less output when hitting them. Unlike the other two protector labs, this one has 20 levels. Description
Protector labs modify the stats of protector enemies while a round is running. One side effect worth knowing: once you start researching enemy-stat labs, the usual base ratios between enemy types stop holding true. For example, boss health is normally a fixed multiple of basic enemy health, but if you have researched basic health reduction, that multiple no longer applies. Keep this in mind when comparing enemy stats or planning around specific breakpoints.
