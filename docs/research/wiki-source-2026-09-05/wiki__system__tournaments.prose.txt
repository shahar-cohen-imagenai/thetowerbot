Tournaments are competitive events where you and 29 other players in the same league each submit a run, and your highest wave decides your placement. Finishing well pays out Gems and Power Stones — the main way to earn the stones you need for Ultimate Weapons — and the top league also awards Keys for Vault upgrades.
There are six leagues: Copper, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Champion and Legend. Higher leagues mean tougher enemy scaling, more battle conditions, and better rewards.

Description
A tournament brackets you with 30 players from your league, and everyone is ranked by the highest wave they reach. Payouts are Gems and Power Stones based on your final position, with Legend league adding Keys on top. Since Power Stones buy Ultimate Weapons, tournaments are their primary source, so placing well here directly speeds up your long-term progression.
Entering A Tournament
Tournaments unlock through Milestones at tier 1 wave 60. Entering costs 1 Tournament Ticket per run, and the highest wave of that run is what gets submitted. Your wave total is compared against the rest of the bracket to set your position and rewards.
You cannot join a tournament while a run is active, so finish or end your current run first. Tournaments run every Wednesday and Saturday at 0:00 AM UTC.
Getting Tickets
You receive one free ticket each time a tournament begins. If you want more attempts, extra tickets come from watching an ad or paying gems — 10 gems for the first, with the price rising by another 10 gems for each additional ticket you buy.
Promoting
As of v25, the top 4 finishers in each bracket move up to the next league. From Platinum onward, the bottom 6 are demoted to the league below. Copper, Silver and Gold are protected tiers — once you have climbed out of Copper or Silver, you cannot be dropped back into them.
Tournament Difficulty
Leagues are described as "tier +", where the plus signals that enemies scale harder than they do in the normal tier of the same name. Two things create that extra difficulty: enemy stats ramp up faster, and Heat (Battle Conditions) adds modifiers on top.
Enemy Stat Scaling
Each league sits between two normal tiers in raw stats. Copper is harder than Tier 1 but easier than Tier 2, and the pattern continues up to Champion, which lands between Tier 11 and Tier 12. Every stat scales faster than usual, including enemy mass — which affects knockback — and enemy speed.
Battle Conditions
Every league except Copper applies Heat, a set of modifiers that make tournament runs play differently from normal ones. Heat starts weak and grows stronger as you clear waves, hitting full strength at wave 1000.
The exception is the "More bosses" condition, which is an Overheat effect — it stays fixed rather than scaling with waves. Copper spawns a boss every 10 waves, Silver every 9, Gold every 8, and so on down to every 5 waves in Legend.
List of Battle conditions
From Platinum up, either the Death Defy or Energy Shield condition is always active. On top of that, Silver adds one random battle condition, Gold adds two, and the count rises by one per league until Legend, which runs five at once. Lab: Battle Condition Reduction
This lab unlocks at Tier 18 Wave 1000 and reduces the effect of all active battle conditions by a percentage per level, across 10 levels. It does not apply to the Ultimate Weapon Duration battle condition. The table below shows the reduction per level.
Lab: Battle Conditions Group 1
Unlocked at Tier 19 Wave 100. Each battle condition in this group has its own lab that reduces that condition's effect by a percentage per level, with 20 levels each. The group covers Knockback Resistance, Thorns Resistance, Orb Resistance, Plasma Cannon Resistance and Death Ray Resistance.

Unlocked at Tier 19 Wave 500. Each condition in this group has its own lab reducing its effect by a percentage per level, with 10 levels each.
