Version 0.11 introduced several new gameplay systems, including Demon Mode, Wave Skip, and Critical Chance, alongside a Tier 5 lab research for card presets and one additional max card slot. Smart missiles received notable buffs to base damage, per-level scaling, and missile count. The update also fixed a range of issues, from Second Wind failing against ranged units to store pricing errors, iOS crashes, and incorrect upgrade costs from buy multipliers.
