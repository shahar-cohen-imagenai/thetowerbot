Starting Cash is a workshop upgrade that boosts how much cash you have available when a round begins. This gives you a head start on purchasing upgrades and defenses right from the start.
Description
Starting Cash increases the amount of cash you begin each round with. This gives you more resources to spend on upgrades and defenses from the moment a round starts, helping you establish your build faster.
Lab
Starting Cash can be further enhanced through the lab system. The lab upgrade is unlocked through Milestones once you reach tier 1 wave 30. It has 99 levels available for research.
