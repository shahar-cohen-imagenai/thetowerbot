Range is a common card in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense that extends how far your tower can reach. Equipping it increases your tower's range, letting you engage enemies from a greater distance.
Range
Range is a common card whose effect reads: Increase tower range by [x]. When equipped, it extends the distance at which your tower can target enemies, which matters when you want your defenses to start dealing damage sooner or cover a wider area. Card Mastery
The Range card mastery costs 750 stones to unlock. Once unlocked, it adds a damage per meter bonus multiplier, so your tower deals more damage based on how far it is hitting. This bonus only applies while the Range card is equipped in your active loadout.
