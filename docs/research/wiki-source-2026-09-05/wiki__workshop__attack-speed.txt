Attack Speed determines how many projectiles your tower fires over time. It also raises the travel speed of those projectiles, including Energy Net and Plasma Cannon shots, until you complete the Light Speed Shots lab research.
The value shown in game is not a straight shots-per-second figure. At low values it roughly matches projectiles per second before rapid fire, multishot and bounce shot are counted, but each point contributes less as the value climbs, and above 40 the returns diminish further. Attack Speed received performance changes in V27 and its exact behaviour is still being analysed by the community.

Description
Attack Speed is the number of projectiles fired from your tower, and it also increases how quickly those projectiles travel to their target, including Energy Net and Plasma Cannon shots.
At very low values the stat is close to shots fired per second, before rapid fire, multishot and bounce shot are factored in. At medium values it behaves more like shots per two seconds, and once the value passes 40 each additional point gives even less. The stat changed in V27 for performance reasons and is still under analysis, so displayed values may not perfectly match real fire rates.
Once you finish the Light Speed Shots lab research, your projectiles move at the maximum possible speed and their travel time is no longer tied to Attack Speed. Energy Net and Plasma Cannon are unaffected by this change.
Workshop
Attack Speed is available in the workshop for free from the start. It has 99 levels, beginning at a base of 1.00 and gaining 0.05 per level, for a maximum workshop value of 5.95.
Formula
The game uses the unrounded result of this calculation, so avoid rounding any intermediate values.
Displayed Attack Speed = (Workshop x Lab x Card + Module Sub Effect) x Enhancement

Cards
The Attack Speed card is a common card available from the start of the game. When equipped, its multiplier applies to the workshop value only. It does not multiply module sub effects.

Lab Upgrades — Light Speed Shots
Light Speed Shots is unlocked through Milestones at tier 7 wave 10. Once researched, your projectiles travel to their target instantly after being fired.
A shot can still be intercepted: if an untargeted enemy sits between your tower and the target, that enemy blocks the shot and takes the damage instead. Research costs are shown in the table below.
