Bot Bot is a support bot that boosts the effects of your other bots while they are inside its radius. It does nothing on its own, so its value scales with how many other bots you run and how strong they are.

Description
While active, Bot Bot increases the effects of your other bots as long as they are within its radius. It is purely a support bot: it has no effect by itself and only pays off when paired with at least one other bot.
Unlocking/Upgrading
As with every bot, you unlock and upgrade Bot Bot through the Event Shop menu. The medals needed to unlock it depend on how many other bots you have already bought. Each of its four upgrades begins at a base cost of 100 medals, rising by 40 medals per level.
Duration
Duration controls how long Bot Bot stays active each time it fires. It starts at 20 seconds and can be raised through 30 levels, with each level adding 0.5 seconds of active time.
Cooldown
Cooldown sets the gap between activations. It begins at 120 seconds and has 15 levels, each one shaving 3 seconds off the wait.
Bonus
Bonus is the multiplier Bot Bot applies to the other bots inside its range. It starts at x1.05 and has 30 levels, with each level adding 0.5 to the multiplier.
Range
Range sets how far Bot Bot's effect reaches while it is active, and it is amplified by your tower range. It starts at 25m and has 18 levels, each adding 2m.
