Super Critical gives your tower a chance to deal additional critical damage whenever a critical hit lands. It builds on top of your existing crit stats rather than replacing them, so it only matters once your critical hit chance is reasonably high.
The system has two parts: a chance to trigger the super crit, and a multiplier that decides how hard it hits. Both are upgraded through the Workshop and expanded further through labs.

Description
A super critical is a follow-up roll that happens only after your tower lands a normal critical hit. If the super crit roll succeeds, the hit deals extra damage based on the Super Crit Multiplier. If your crit chance is low, super crits will trigger rarely, so this upgrade scales with how often you already crit.
Workshop
Super Critical is unlocked in the Workshop for 100 million coins, after Bounce Shot. Unlocking it opens two upgrade tracks: Super Crit Chance and Super Crit Mult.
Super Crit Chance
Super Crit Chance is the probability of a super crit occurring, checked only when a critical hit succeeds. The Workshop track has 100 levels, each adding 0.2%, for a maximum of 20.00% from the Workshop.
Workshop — Super Crit Mult
Super Crit Mult is the damage multiplier applied when a super crit lands. The Workshop offers 120 upgrades, starting from a base multiplier of 1.2 and increasing by 0.1 per level, up to a maximum of 13.2. When a super crit triggers, your total crit multiplier is Critical Factor multiplied by Super Crit Mult.
