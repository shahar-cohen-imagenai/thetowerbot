Bits are one of the guild-linked currencies in The Tower: Idle Tower Defense. Their entire purpose is feeding the Chip system: you spend them to unlock and upgrade Chips and to open additional Chip Slots, so your Bit income directly limits how fast your Chips develop.

Bits
Bits are a dedicated currency with a single focus: the Chip system. You spend them to unlock and upgrade Chips, and to purchase extra Chip Slots. Because they have no other use, every Bit you earn goes toward strengthening your Chips in some way.
Sources
You get Bits from two places, both tied to guilds. The first is Guild Weekly Reward Chests, which pay out Bits based on your weekly results. The second is the Guild Store, where you can buy Bits with Tokens. Guild Store stock resets when a new Guild Season begins, so each season gives you a fresh set of purchases.
Weekly Reward Chest Breakdown
If you hit the maximum weekly reward total every week, you build up roughly 740 Bits over a four-week month and about 1,480 Bits across a full eight-week Guild Season. The table below shows how the chest rewards break down by week.

Uses
Bits have exactly two uses: upgrading Chips you already own and buying additional Chip Slots so you can equip more Chips at once. The cost table below details what each upgrade and slot costs.
