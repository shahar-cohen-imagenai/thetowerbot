Defense Percent cuts a percentage off every hit you take, and the reduction is worked out before Defense Absolute is subtracted. Because it scales with the size of incoming damage, it becomes one of the most valuable defensive stats as enemy damage grows.
Although the sources listed below can add up to more than 100%, the stat is hard-capped at 98%, so you can never become fully immune through Defense Percent alone.

Description
Defense Percent is the share of incoming damage that gets removed before anything else happens. The game applies this percentage reduction first, then subtracts Defense Absolute from whatever is left, so the two stats stack multiplicatively in effect rather than competing.
The cap matters here: even if your combined bonuses exceed 100%, the game only honours 98%. Any investment past that point is wasted, so it pays to know where your total stands.

Workshop
Defense Percent is bought in the Workshop for 75 coins. It sits in the Defense Absolute line, which itself opens up after you have unlocked Health and Health Regen.
The Workshop offers 99 upgrades for this stat, each adding 0.5% to the reduction. Fully maxed, the Workshop alone gives you 49.5% Defense Percent.

Card
The Extra Defense card stacks directly on top of your Workshop value. Combining the Workshop maximum, the Defense % lab, the Extra Defense card and the Defense % perk can push the total to 95.5% before relics, leaving only a small gap to the 98% limit.
Not every form of damage reduction feeds into this cap. Chrono Field Damage Reduction, Primordial Collapse and Flame Bot are all separate mechanics: they do not count toward the 98%, and their reductions are calculated after Defense Absolute rather than before it.
Defense Percent
The Defense Percent lab unlocks through Milestones at tier 5, wave 30. It has 50 levels, and whatever it provides is added straight onto your Workshop total.
You do not need to finish the lab to hit the cap. With Workshop upgrades at +49.50%, the Extra Defense card at +11%, perks at +25% and Sub-Module effects at +8%, the 98% ceiling is reachable by level 23 of the lab, which contributes +4.60%. Levels beyond that only matter if you are missing one of the other sources.

Relics
Four relics grant additive Defense % bonuses, and each one counts toward the 98% hard cap. Three of them give 1% each while the fourth gives 2%, adding up to a maximum of 5% from relics.
Because relic bonuses share the cap with everything else, they are most useful while your lab or Workshop is still incomplete. Once the rest of your setup reaches 98% on its own, these relics stop providing any practical benefit.
