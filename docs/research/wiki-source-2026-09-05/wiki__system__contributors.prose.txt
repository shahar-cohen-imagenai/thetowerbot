This page brings together the resources used to keep the wiki up to date. You will find action lists, contact details, information sources, editing help, and tools, all aimed at making it easier for contributors to update and improve wiki content.
Action List
The action list tracks wiki content that needs updating or adding. Entries may come straight from patch notes or from revisiting older pages that need attention. If you spot something missing that belongs here, add it yourself.
Items on the list are generally small jobs that should take under 20 minutes each. There is no formal project leader by design, so if you see a task you want to handle, volunteer for it and take ownership. The table below lists the current open actions.
