Land Mines are a defensive workshop upgrade line that gives enemies a chance to drop a mine, which then explodes and damages everything caught in its blast. The whole line is built around three workshop stats — Chance, Damage and Radius — supported by a card, a workshop enhancement and two lab researches.

Overview
Land Mines are a defense-side workshop feature. When the effect triggers, an enemy drops a mine on the ground, and once that mine goes off it damages every enemy inside its explosion range. How often mines appear, how hard they hit and how wide the blast reaches are all upgraded separately in the workshop.
Land Mine Chance
Land Mine Chance is the probability that an enemy drops a land mine. Raising it means more mines on the field over a run.
Land Mine Chance — Workshop
Land Mine Chance is unlocked in the workshop for 400,000 coins, bundled with Land Mine Damage and Land Mine Radius, and becomes available after Shockwave Size and Shockwave Frequency. It has 50 upgrade levels, each adding 0.60% to the spawn chance, for a maximum of 30.00%. Once a mine has spawned it will explode on its own after a fixed delay, and that delay can be extended through the Land Mine Decay lab.
Land Mine Damage
Land Mine Damage sets how much of your tower's damage a mine deals when it explodes, expressed as a percentage. The calculation already factors in your critical and super critical multipliers and their chances.
Land Mine Damage — Workshop
Land Mine Damage is unlocked in the workshop for 400,000 coins together with Land Mine Chance and Land Mine Radius, after Shockwave Size and Shockwave Frequency. It offers 200 upgrade levels starting from a base value of 100%, with each level adding 10%, reaching a maximum of 2100% damage.
Card
The Land Mine Stun card adds a control effect to your mines: when one explodes, it stuns any non-boss enemies caught in the blast range. Bosses are immune to the stun, but it still buys breathing room against regular waves.
Damage Calculation
Mine damage uses your average critical factor and average super critical factor, weighted by their activation chances. The full formula is:
Land Mine Damage = Land Mine Damage % * Damage * (1 + Critical Factor * Critical Chance) * (1 + Super Crit Mult * Super Crit Chance * Crit Chance)
Land Mine Radius
Land Mine Radius determines the size of the explosion when a mine goes off. A larger radius means each mine can hit more enemies at once.
Land Mine Radius — Workshop
Land Mine Radius is unlocked in the workshop for 400,000 coins alongside Land Mine Chance and Land Mine Damage, after Shockwave Size and Shockwave Frequency. It has 50 upgrade levels, which together add up to a total of 1.50 extra radius.
