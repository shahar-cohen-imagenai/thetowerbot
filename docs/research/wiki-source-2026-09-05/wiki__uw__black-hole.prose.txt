Black Hole is an Ultimate Weapon that periodically opens a gravity well near your tower, dragging enemies towards its centre for as long as it stays active. It is one of the strongest crowd-control tools in the game, grouping enemies so your other damage sources can hit them together.
You unlock and upgrade it from the Ultimate Weapon menu, and a set of lab researches later add damage, coin bonuses and extra effects on top.

Description
Once activated, Black Hole spawns after a set delay, holds enemies within its range for a fixed duration, then disappears until its cooldown completes. While active it continuously pulls enemies towards its centre, keeping them clustered and away from your tower.
Where the Black Hole appears depends on your tower range. A larger tower range pushes the spawn point further out, and the distance grows faster than the range itself, so at high range the centre of the Black Hole sits well outside your tower range line.

Workshop — Ultimate Weapon Plus
Ultimate Weapon Plus abilities become available only after you have purchased all 9 Ultimate Weapons. The Black Hole Plus ability makes each Black Hole deal a multiple of the current wave's HP to every enemy it affected when its activation ends.

Labs — Black Hole Damage
This research makes Black Hole deal damage equal to a percentage of each enemy's total health per second while they are inside it. Protectors do not reduce this damage, which makes it one of the few reliable ways to hurt protected enemies.

Labs — Black Hole Coin Bonus
This research awards bonus coins for enemies killed while inside a Black Hole. The kill does not need to come from the Black Hole itself, and the enemy does not even need to be caught by its pull — it only needs to be within the Black Hole when it dies.

Labs — Extra Black Hole
This research spawns a second Black Hole whenever your Black Hole activates. The second one always appears on the opposite side of the tower and copies every stat and ability of the original, including any damage, coin bonus and Ultimate Weapon Plus effects you have unlocked.
Doubling your coverage this way dramatically improves both crowd control and coin income. Costs per level are listed in the table below.
Labs — Black Hole Disable Ranged Enemies
With this research, ranged enemies caught inside a Black Hole cannot fire, even if your tower is within their attack range. This removes one of the main threats during late-tier runs, since ranged enemies are normally able to shoot while being pulled.
