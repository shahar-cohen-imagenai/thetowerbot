Inner Land Mines is an ultimate weapon that periodically deploys a set of mines around your tower. When an enemy touches one, it detonates and deals area-of-effect damage calculated as a percentage of your tower's damage, with crit and super crit chance and multipliers factored in.
The weapon's activation cooldown has a hard cap of 37 seconds, so no amount of upgrading will push it below that point.

Workshop — Basic Upgrades
Once you unlock Inner Land Mines, its basic upgrades become available in the Ultimate Weapon menu. You can spend stones on three tracks: Damage, Quantity, and Cooldown.

Workshop — Ultimate Weapon Plus
Charged Mine becomes available once you have unlocked all 9 ultimate weapons. It causes each Inner Land Mine's damage to grow by a multiplier every second, and the charge only resets when that mine actually detonates.
The multiplier is tracked per mine, so when one mine explodes, the others keep whatever charge they had built up. Active mines also retain their charge when the weapon's cooldown resets and a new set spawns.

Labs — Inner Mine Stun
With this lab researched, exploding inner mines stun nearby enemies for 2.5 seconds. It has a single level.
Labs — Inner Land Mine - Chrono Jump
This lab requires ILM+ (Charged Mine). When an enemy is hit by an Inner Land Mine, that mine's charge is boosted by this lab's value multiplied by the number of times that enemy has been hit by an Inner Land Mine. It has 10 levels; per-level values are listed in the table below.
