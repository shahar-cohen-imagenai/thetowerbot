Vampire labs let you weaken Vampire enemies directly by researching permanent modifiers to their stats. Each lab targets a specific part of how Vampires behave in combat, so their value depends on how much trouble this enemy type gives you.

Description — Vampire Enemy Attack

Description — Vampire Enemy Health
This lab cuts the maximum health of Vampire enemies by a percentage, letting you kill them faster. It unlocks at Tier 19 Wave 60 and has 30 levels of research.
Description — Garlic Thorns
Garlic Thorns unlocks at Tier 2 Wave 1000 and has 10 upgrades. Once researched, Vampire elites take a percentage of your thorns damage per second while they are using their ranged attack, punishing them for staying at range. The damage percentage per upgrade level is listed in the table below.

Description
Vampire labs alter the stats of Vampire enemies during rounds. Once you research one of these labs, the base stat relationships between enemies stop holding true. For example, a boss will no longer always have twenty times the health of a basic enemy if you have researched basic enemy health reductions.
